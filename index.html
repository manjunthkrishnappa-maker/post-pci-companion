The Pathological Recovery Phase

It is important to understand that while a PCI (stent) restores blood flow, the biological healing of the heart tissue is an active pathological process that continues for approximately 30 days post-insult. Even if you feel normal, your heart is undergoing critical recovery. This assistant provides the active monitoring necessary to catch early warning signs during this vital window.
